07

Series vs Parallel

Compare the two fundamental circuit arrangements.

🔗

Series

  • Same current flows through components.
  • Total resistance increases.
  • Voltage divides across components.
  • One break can stop the circuit.
🌐

Parallel

  • Same voltage across branches.
  • Current divides between branches.
  • Equivalent resistance decreases.
  • Branches can operate independently.
