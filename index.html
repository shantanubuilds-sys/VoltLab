What is Ohm's Law?

Ohm's Law describes the relationship between voltage, current and resistance.

V = I × R

Current can be calculated using:

I = V ÷ R

How Does a Series Circuit Work?

In a series circuit, the same current flows through every resistor.

R Total = R1 + R2 + R3

The voltage is divided across the resistors. A larger resistance receives a larger voltage drop.
